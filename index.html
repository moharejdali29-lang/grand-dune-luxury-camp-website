Questions, answered

Before you
arrive.

A few useful details for planning your stay at Grand Dune Luxury Camp.

Where is Grand Dune Luxury Camp located?

Grand Dune Luxury Camp is located in Merzouga, near the Erg Chebbi dunes in southeastern Morocco, offering a peaceful Sahara desert experience surrounded by the golden dunes.

How many tents does Grand Dune Luxury Camp have?

The camp has 6 private tents across different accommodation options, including double, single, triple, quadruple and five-person tents.

What types of tents are available?

The camp offers accommodation for solo travelers, couples, families and groups, with single, double, triple, quadruple and five-person tent options.

How much does a stay at Grand Dune Luxury Camp cost?

Rates start from €35 per person. The final price may depend on the accommodation and booking details. Contact us directly for the current rate for your stay.

Can I contact the camp through WhatsApp?

Yes. You can contact Grand Dune Luxury Camp directly through WhatsApp at +212 651 398 214 to ask questions or request a booking.

What can I do during my stay in the Merzouga desert?

Depending on your booking and arrangements, you can enjoy experiences such as camel trekking, watching the sunset and sunrise over the Erg Chebbi dunes, traditional Moroccan tea, desert dining and relaxing under the Sahara sky.

Is Grand Dune Luxury Camp suitable for couples and families?

Yes. With several tent sizes available, the camp can accommodate couples, solo travelers, families and small groups.

How can I book my stay?

You can contact Grand Dune Luxury Camp directly through WhatsApp at +212 651 398 214 or by email at moharejdali29@gmail.com.

Is the camp in the Erg Chebbi desert?

Yes. Grand Dune Luxury Camp is located in the Merzouga area near the famous Erg Chebbi dunes in Morocco.

What should I bring for a Sahara desert stay?

Bring comfortable clothing, sun protection, comfortable footwear, a light jacket for cooler evenings and any personal essentials you may need during your desert stay.
